Circular Economy

Designing systems where resources are reused and regenerated

Key Principles

Resource Efficiency

Circular economy focuses on reducing waste and using resources more efficiently. Materials are reused, repaired and recycled instead of being discarded.

Sustainable Production

Products are designed to last longer and be reused or recycled. This reduces environmental impact and promotes responsible production.

Future Development

Circular systems create long-term environmental and economic stability by reducing resource extraction and waste generation.

The Circular Cycle

Reduce ↓ Reuse ↓ Repair ↓ Recycle ↓ Regenerate
