Our Vision

A Circular System
Built for Mongolia

MCBI was founded on a simple but powerful belief: the resources inside used batteries should return to the earth's economy — not poison its water and soil.

🏭

Local Recycling Infrastructure

We are building Mongolia's first dedicated battery collection and processing system, reducing reliance on unregulated disposal.

🤝

Czech Republic Pilot Partnership

Our collaboration with Czech engineers and researchers brings proven European recycling technologies to a Mongolian context.

🔋

Critical Mineral Recovery

Lithium, cobalt, nickel, and manganese recovered from spent batteries re-enter supply chains — creating local economic value.

The Problem We Solve

Why It Matters

The global energy transition generates millions of spent batteries each year. Without systems to manage them, the cost is paid by communities, rivers, and soil.

01
💧

Clean Water at Risk

Improperly disposed batteries leach lead, cadmium, and acid into groundwater, threatening drinking sources and aquatic ecosystems across Mongolia's vast steppe.

02
🌿

Soil Contamination

Heavy metal contamination from battery waste degrades agricultural land and nomadic herding territories — a direct threat to livelihoods and food security.

03
⛏️

Critical Mineral Waste

Mongolia holds extraordinary mineral wealth. Battery recycling can recover lithium, cobalt, and nickel — keeping strategic resources in local hands rather than landfills.

04
⚡

The EV Battery Wave

The global electric vehicle revolution will retire hundreds of millions of batteries in the next decade. Countries without recycling systems will become dumping grounds. Countries with them will build wealth.

MCBI positions Mongolia on the right side of this transition — as a processing and recovery nation, not a disposal site.

140M
EVs on roads by 2035
(IEA Estimate)
05
🔄

Circular Economy Logic

Every kilogram of battery waste properly recycled is a kilogram of new material that doesn't need to be mined, shipped, or refined from scratch. Circularity is efficiency.
