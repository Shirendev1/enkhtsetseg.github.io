# Future Thinking – Mongolia as a Global Model

Future Thinking is an open concept initiative exploring how Mongolia can become
a global example where human values, nature, and technology coexist in harmony.
## Vision

To demonstrate that Mongolia can evolve into a global model by aligning:
- Human-centered values
- Environmental sustainability
- Advanced digital and energy infrastructure
  ## Core Idea

From Energy Consumer → Digital Infrastructure Competitor

Key pillars:
- Renewable energy
- Energy storage (key pillar)
- Green data centers
- Cold-climate advantage
